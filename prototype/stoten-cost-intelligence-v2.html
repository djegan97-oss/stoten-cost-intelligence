⚖ Normalization Methodology
Size normalization: All $/SF metrics are calculated on gross building SF, enabling like-for-like comparison across buildings of different footprints.
Clear height premium: Each foot of clear height above the 28-ft baseline adds an estimated $0.45/SF to structural and mechanical costs. The "Height-Adj Cost/SF" strips this out to compare buildings at equivalent ceiling heights.
Site premium: Land and site development costs are isolated and shown per acre. Buildings on larger or more constrained sites will show higher site cost/acre.
Dock door cost: Total actual cost divided by number of loading dock doors — a useful trade-level proxy for dock-intensive facilities.

Normalization Standards
· All costs are all-in (GC fee, GCs, insurance, bonds)
· Clear height baseline: 28 ft · Premium: $0.45/SF per additional ft
· Site premium baseline: gross improved acres including truck court
· No owner soft costs (design, permitting, financing) in database
