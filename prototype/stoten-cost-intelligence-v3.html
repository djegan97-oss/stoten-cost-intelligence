⚖ Normalization Methodology
Size normalization: All $/SF metrics are calculated on gross building SF, enabling like-for-like comparison across buildings of different footprints.
Clear height premium: Each foot of clear height above the 28-ft baseline adds an estimated $0.45/SF to structural and mechanical costs. The "Height-Adj Cost/SF" strips this out to compare buildings at equivalent ceiling heights.
Site premium: Land and site development costs are isolated and shown per acre. Buildings on larger or more constrained sites will show higher site cost/acre.
Dock door cost: Total actual cost divided by number of loading dock doors — a useful trade-level proxy for dock-intensive facilities.

Methodology
How v3 derives unit rates, applies adjustments, and decides confidence.
Demo · Sample Data

Two-layer architecture

v3 separates math from analysis. Math runs in deterministic JavaScript — every dollar value, percentage, and delta is the result of explicit arithmetic on the historical project data, not an AI guess. AI is layered on top as commentary, push-back scripts, and free-form Q&A — it sees the deterministic numbers as input and writes plain English as output.

Unit rate derivation

For each of the 10 trade categories (the Schedule of Values), we compute the average $/SF across all 10 historical Stoten projects: unit_rate = average(trade_cost / building_sf). We also compute the standard deviation, low, and high — those drive the ±1σ bands on the Bid Compare panel.

Adjustments applied to each estimate

  • Building type — cold storage adds 100% to mech and 40% to panels; cross-dock adds 45% to dock equip and 18% to site/civil; flex reduces dock equip 45% but lifts MEP for office build-out.
  • Clear height — $0.45/SF per ft over 28', allocated only to height-sensitive trades (concrete, steel, panels carry most of it; roofing and electrical are flat).
  • Region — Midwest +6%, Southeast −4%, Southwest −3%, South Central −7% from the national average.
  • Building size — fixed-cost effect: under 80K SF +6%, 80–150K SF baseline, over 150K SF −3%.
  • FAR — site/civil scales inversely with FAR. Lower FAR (bigger site per SF of building) → higher site/civil.
  • Dock count — dock equipment scales with how many doors you specify versus the portfolio average doors-per-100K SF.
  • Fire tank — fixed $150K adder to plumbing/FP when toggled.

Confidence model

For each estimate we find comparable projects by scoring matches on building type (4 pts), within-30% size (3 pts), within-4ft clear height (2 pts), and same region (2 pts). Projects scoring 5+ count as comps. Confidence is high with 5 comps, moderate with 3-4, limited with 1-2, and "no close comps — using portfolio average" with 0.

What v3 v0.1 deliberately doesn't do yet

  • Save / name / share specific estimates (URL-state coming next)
  • Per-trade manual overrides (e.g. "force mech to $1.85/SF")
  • Auto-suggest from a project name ("estimate for Cat Drive")
  • Real Stoten data ingest — still using 10 illustrative projects
  • Adjustment-factor tuning UI — those constants are educated guesses for v0.1, will be recalibrated when Jack pulls real closeouts
  • PDF export of an estimate

AI plumbing

Out of the box, every AI feature uses a templated fallback — rule-based commentary that varies with inputs but isn't generative. Click the gear icon (top-right of any estimator panel) and paste an Anthropic API key to switch to live AI via claude-sonnet-4-6. Your key stays in your browser's localStorage and is only sent to Anthropic when you trigger an AI feature. In production this moves to a Supabase Edge Function with the key server-side.

Normalization Standards
· All costs are all-in (GC fee, GCs, insurance, bonds)
· Clear height baseline: 28 ft · Premium: $0.45/SF per additional ft
· Site premium baseline: gross improved acres including truck court
· No owner soft costs (design, permitting, financing) in database
